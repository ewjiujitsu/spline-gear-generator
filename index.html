06 GO / NO-GO Gauges
Max. effective tooth thickness — GO gauge [mm]
s_v max · blank = use actual maxi
Gauge root ↔ part tip clearance [mm]
c (blank = 0.25)
GO = FULL-FORM mating profile at the MAX EFFECTIVE limit (enter t_v max / s_v min from the sheet; blank falls back to the ACTUAL limit = no form allowance, flagged in the report). NO-GO = mating profile at the MIN ACTUAL limit — make it a SECTOR gauge (2 opposing sectors, ~2 teeth) so index errors can't mask it; the DXF is the full profile. Gauge teeth reach the part FORM ∅; gauge root clears the part tip ∅ by c; involute held true over the full engagement. Gauge-maker tolerance + wear allowance go INTO the part zone.
07 Coating — pre / post coating stages
Coating [-]

Coating thickness, mini — PER SURFACE [mm]
c mini (on the surface normal)
Coating thickness, maxi — PER SURFACE [mm]
c maxi (on the surface normal)
PER SURFACE — enter the coating spec as written (e.g. 0.010–0.025). A thickness c on the surface normal moves EVERY ∅ by 2c and the base tangent by ΔW = 2c; at the pitch ∅ the s/t band moves 2c/cos αp. Flanks, root ∅ AND tip ∅ are all treated as coated.
The two stages do NOT share a tolerance band. Because c varies, the PRE-coating band is narrower than the drawing band by 2×(c maxi − c mini)/cos αp — that is what guarantees the finished part is in tolerance for ANY coating thickness in spec. Each pre limit is paired with the coating extreme that works AGAINST it: pre maxi assumes the thinnest coating, pre mini assumes the thickest. So a part anywhere in the pre band, given any coating in spec, lands inside the drawing band. If the coating variation eats the whole band, the two specs are incompatible and the tool says so instead of quietly clamping.
Geometry is cut at the MID of each stage's own band (= drawing mid −/+ 2c̄). The tooth-tip BREAK (radius/chamfer) is carried across unchanged — it is an edge break, not a functional dimension, and a coated sharp corner is not well defined.

Tolerance treatment [-]
USE THIS WHEN THE FEATURE IS CUT SOFT AND NOT MACHINED AFTER HT. If it is hard-finished after HT the distortion is absorbed by the grinding stock — use the soft-stage allowance in section 05 instead, and increase it if the distortion exceeds it. The two are alternatives, never both on the same feature.
DIRECTION — GROWTH moves every surface outward along its own normal. On an INTERNAL spline that makes the space width and every ∅ smaller, so the pre-HT profile must be cut bigger. SHRINKAGE is the mirror image. A distortion d per surface moves any ∅ by 2d and the base tangent by ΔW = 2d; at the pitch ∅ the s/t band moves 2d/cos αp. Flanks, root ∅ AND tip ∅ are all treated as distorted.
MINI may be NEGATIVE — measure ten parts and some may move the other way. A negative mini simply means "some parts went against the direction set above".
NARROW THE BAND pairs each pre-HT limit with the distortion extreme that works AGAINST it, so a part anywhere in the pre-HT band lands inside the drawing band for ANY distortion in the measured range — the band narrows by 2×(d maxi − d mini)/cos αp. That is what makes a whole batch come out right. If the scatter eats the whole band the tool says so and points you at SHIFT THE MID rather than quietly clamping.
SHIFT THE MID keeps the band width and moves the mid by the mean distortion. The scatter is still measured and printed as a % of the band, but it is not applied — individual parts can then fall outside. Use it when the scatter will not fit and you need the aim point anyway.
HONEST LIMIT — this is a SIZE compensation only. It does not model out-of-round, lead/helix distortion or index error, which are usually the larger half of real HT movement and cannot be corrected by a size shift. And the band is only as good as the sample it came from: ten parts is a sample, not a specification — the eleventh can fall outside it.
The tooth-tip BREAK is carried across unchanged — it is an edge break, not a functional dimension.
